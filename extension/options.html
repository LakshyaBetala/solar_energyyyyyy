Solara co-pilot

Fills the government portals from your Solara records

Connection

In Solara, open Connect extension (top right). It shows the address and creates a key.

How it works for you

Turn these on or off. You always type the OTP and the CAPTCHA yourself.

Fill each page as soon as it opensAfter you pick a customer and press Start, forms fill by themselves.
Answer “Yes” to “vendor fills the form”The vendor fills the application for the customer.
Show the classic side panelThe island does everything. Turn this on only if you also want the old panel.
Press Save, Upload, Next and Submit for meOnly when a page is completely filled and the OTP, CAPTCHA and declaration are done by the person.

Speed

How quickly the co-pilot fills a form. It always stops when everything is filled and waits for you to press Next or Submit.

Where things appear

Move the top island and the side panel to suit your screen. Changes apply straight away.

First time?

  1. Sign in to Solara and press Connect extension.
  2. Copy the address and the key into the boxes above, then Save and test.
  3. Open a portal, pick the customer in the Solara panel, press Start.
Your key stays in this browser only.
