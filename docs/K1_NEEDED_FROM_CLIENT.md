# Milestone 1: what we need from you

Solara's part of Milestone 1 is built and tested on our test copy of the portals. On that copy, a new customer goes all the way through in one sitting:
1. Their documents are read.
2. They are registered.
3. They are accepted in the vendor login.
4. They sign the agreement on their phone.
5. The agreement is uploaded.

To finish Milestone 1 on the real PM Surya Ghar portal, we need three things from you: some decisions, some details, and one hour on the real portal. Each item below says why it matters.

## 1. Decisions

| # | What we need to know | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | **Is the agreement wording approved?** Solara prints it from a draft format, marked DRAFT. Please read it and send changes, or confirm it as it is. Also tell us whether you want a Marathi version. | No real customer should sign a draft. |
| D2 | **Do you sell fixed packages at fixed prices** (for example "3 kW, Rs …, including taxes")? If yes, send the list. | With a list, the cost is filled when you tap the kW. Without one, the cost stays the one figure you type for each customer, from your quote. |
| D3 | **Can the kW you tap and the cost from your quote count as "checked by you"** for the Milestone 1 target "nothing typed except OTPs, CAPTCHA and flagged fields"? | This decides how the target is measured. |
| D4 | **Where should Solara run:** online (hosted in India), or on your office laptop? | Customers open their document link and their signing link from their own phones, so Solara has to be reachable from the internet. On a laptop alone, those two links will not open for the customer. |
| D5 | **What do you do today when a customer has no email?** | PM Surya Ghar sends a code to the customer's email during registration. |

## 2. Details and files

| # | What to send | What it is used for |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | Your company's name exactly as PM Surya Ghar shows it in the vendor list, plus the registered address and GST number | The agreement, and finding your company in the portal's vendor list |
| F2 | The name of the person who signs agreements for the company. They also sign once on Solara's screen | Placed on every agreement, next to the customer's signature |
| F3 | The panel type you usually install (for example Mono PERC, DCR), and the system sizes you usually sell | Filled on the application and shown as the kW buttons |
| F4 | Your current agreement or quotation format, if you have one | So Solara's agreement matches what you already use |
| F5 | 3 to 5 recent bills (the PDF from the MSEDCL website) and Aadhaar photos, from customers who agree to it | To check that Solara reads real documents correctly. They stay on your computer and are not shared |

## 3. Time on the real portal

### Session A: one hour, to see the real pages

- **Who:** you, plus one real new customer who can answer the phone. The portal sends them two codes: one by SMS, one by email.
- **What happens:** you log in and register this customer, then accept them in the vendor login. We watch on a screen share. On each new page, you press one button in the Solara panel that sends us the page's layout: field names only, never what is typed.
- **What we never see:** your password, the OTPs or the CAPTCHA. You type those yourself.
- **Please have ready:**
  - your laptop with Chrome (we install the Solara add-on beforehand; about 10 minutes);
  - your vendor login, and the phone that gets its OTP;
  - the customer's bill and Aadhaar.

### Session B: three customers, timed

Within a week of Session A, we register three real new customers and time your own minutes on each, from "documents in Solara" to "accepted in the vendor login". Time spent waiting for the customer to read out a code is not counted. **The target is 5 minutes or less per customer.**

## 4. Questions we will answer together in Session A

We will note these together during the session. Tell us earlier if you already know:
- Does the real application ask for the roof area, the bank account, or the passbook and roof photo at the start, or only later?
- How does the portal name your electricity company in its list?
- What does "My Application" show as the status after you accept a customer, and after the agreement is uploaded?

## What each item unlocks

| From you | What it unlocks |
|---|---|
| D1, F1, F2, F4 | Real customers can sign the agreement |
| D2, D3, F3 | The Milestone 1 rule "nothing typed by hand" can be measured |
| D4 | Customers can send documents and sign from their own phones |
| D5, F5 | Fewer surprises with real customers |
| Session A | The co-pilot fills the real pages after login |
| Session B | Milestone 1 is complete: 3 real customers, each in 5 minutes or less of your time |
