# Product

## Register

product

## Users

**The owner of a small rooftop-solar installer in Maharashtra.** The owner does the government portal work on a laptop, between site visits and calls with customers, often in a bright office or out on site.

The job on most days: take a customer's electricity bill and Aadhaar and reach "registered on PM Surya Ghar, accepted in the vendor login, agreement uploaded" without typing anything twice. After installation, the owner files the close-out paperwork.

Office staff sometimes enter data, and a supervisor approves. Customers see one screen only: the e-sign page on their phone, in English and Marathi.

## Product Purpose

Solara is the installer's back office. It reads the customer's documents once and flags anything it is not sure about. The co-pilot in the owner's own Chrome fills the government portals; the owner logs in, types the OTPs and CAPTCHAs, and presses every Submit.

Success means two things. First, each customer reaches "agreement uploaded" in about 5 minutes of the owner's time. Second, the owner always knows the one next thing to do for each customer.

## Brand Personality

**Plain, sure, quiet.** Like a well-kept office register: it says what is next and stays out of the way.

Short sentences, using the portal's own words. Specific numbers ("4 of 4 on file", "Rs 2,10,000"), never slogans.

## Anti-references

- A control room full of glowing numbers and status tiles.
- SaaS dashboards built around big-number stat tiles.
- The government portals themselves: dense, link-heavy, every option shown at once.
- Anything that looks like a chatbot or an "AI" product.

## Design Principles

1. **One next step per screen.** The screen leads with what to do now. Everything else is folded or comes after.
2. **Show the values that were read, and explain in one line.** The content is the customer's data; explanations are short.
3. **Flag anything uncertain, with the fix beside it.** Never hide a doubt, and never make the owner hunt for where to correct it.
4. **Readable in daylight at arm's length.** Body text 15px or more, labels in sentence case, contrast AA in both light and dark.
5. **Use the portal's own words**, so the owner recognises each field when the co-pilot fills it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.2 AA in both themes. The owner switches between light and dark, and both must hold.
- Body text at least 15px. Labels at least 13px, never in capitals.
- Full keyboard use, with visible focus.
- Colour is never the only signal: every status also has a word or a mark.
- Reduced motion is respected.
- Marathi (Devanagari) names and messages must render correctly, including the joiner characters used in names.
